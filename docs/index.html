How do I start?

Open the menu and choose a preset world, such as The Hanging Gardens or The Moon Courtyard, then tap Play. Tap Random to generate a new world with its own architecture, key, scale, tempo, and instruments.

How do I build my own world?

Tap Build to open the builder tray. Drag a module onto the world, or tap its card to place it and activate its musical character. Hold a piece and drag to move the whole module; release to confirm. Undo recovers the previous state, and Redo repeats the last undone action.

How do I control the camera?

Pinch to zoom, drag with one finger to rotate, and drag with two fingers to pan. The frame button shows the whole world again. Follow opens controls to track a character, with arrows to switch between active voices; First person puts the camera at that character's eye level.

How do I use the mixer?

Open the mixer to isolate each character and adjust its sound, volume, and pan. Editing pauses playback; tap Play again once you are done.

How do I save or share a world?

Save World keeps the world on your device. Export World saves a complete JSON file you can open on another installation with Import World. Export Stereo WAV saves a musical loop as audio. Share Video renders a vertical video with audio that you can save to Files or send to another app.

Can I edit individual pieces?

From the menu, choose Edit individual pieces for the detailed builder: musical steps, decoration, characters, coordinates, heights, sizes, and mix. Turn off Advanced to return to the simplified module catalog.

Privacy
