# Ocean 360 pilot

Ocean360.mp4 is an adaptation of “3D 360 4K Motion TIme-lapse - Oceanside Beach” (2FILMZ Studios, 2017).

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:3D_360_4K_Motion_TIme-lapse_-_Oceanside_Beach.webm
Original publication: https://vimeo.com/215703486
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original source SHA-1: 4eb70355392d85798848750a690980a9acdce671

Changes: extracted the upper eye from the over/under stereoscopic source; restored a 2:1 monoscopic frame by resizing 3840×1080 to 3840×1920; converted to silent H.264 at 30 fps; selected a short segment and used a one-second temporal crossfade to make an 11-second, 330-frame loop. The vertical resize restores projection proportions; it creates no additional recorded detail. The PNG poster is the first frame of that adaptation. These assets are a 4K coastal/beach time-lapse pilot, not 8K open-ocean sunset footage. Camera translation and capture/stitching limitations remain in the source.
